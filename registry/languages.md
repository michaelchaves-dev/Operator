# Languages — packs, not gateways

One Operator. Languages are rows you add, deepen, or rest.
A country is a marketing lane. A dialect is a pack version. Neither is a new phone system.

## Rule

Ship a language when there is a market or a seat that will use it.
Do not pre-train the planet.

Update the row when needed. Reassign the linguist seat. Do not mint "Marci-Brazil" as a second plane.

## Row

```
id            pt-BR-rio
family        portuguese
market_lane   brazil
variant       rio / informal spoken
status        planned | research | trained | live | rest
register      path or repo for that desk (housing, front-desk, etc.)
officer       marci (craft stays)
notes         what this variant must not mix with
```

`pt-BR` ≠ `pt-PT`. Rio talk-to-text ≠ Lisbon legal register.
If the utterance and the pack disagree, one question: which lane?

## Hard languages

Irish, Scottish Gaelic, and many African language/dialect lanes are **research seats** first:

1. Collect a tiny gold set (real desk phrases, not scraped novels).
2. One specialist loop: lookup + draft in that lect, human review.
3. Promote to `trained` only after the desk can take a live text without inventing law or mixing lects.
4. `live` only on an allowlisted property/market.

Specialized training is a charter on the seat, not a new agent id per lect.

## Marketing vs runtime

Marketing may say: Brazil desk, Gaelic desk, Yoruba desk.
Runtime is still:

`text → Operator → market pack → language row → Marci tools → human gate`

SubtracToken may compress/route the NL path. It does not become the register for that lect.

## Do not

- Mix lects in one reply to look fluent.
- Let a cheap model fill an untrained lect.
- Promise every dialect on the sales page before the row is `live`.
